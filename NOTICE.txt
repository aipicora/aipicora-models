AIPICORA search model files (release search-1)

siglip2_vision_int8.onnx
  The image (vision) encoder of Google SigLIP 2 base, patch 16, 224 px
  (google/siglip2-base-patch16-224), licensed under the Apache License 2.0.
  Int8 ONNX export published by onnx-community on Hugging Face.
  Not modified by AIPICORA. See LICENSE-APACHE-2.0.txt.

search_table_wordnet_1.bin
  Text descriptions (embeddings) of 25,470 English words and phrases taken
  from WordNet 3.0, computed with the matching SigLIP 2 text encoder
  (Apache License 2.0). The word list is a modification of WordNet 3.0.
